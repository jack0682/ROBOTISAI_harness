# Protocol · Writing

> Layer 1 — universal per-task procedure. No project specifics.

Use when producing prose: docs, explanations, summaries, messages.

## Steps
1. **Know the reader and the purpose.** Who reads this, what should they be able to
   do after, how much do they already know.
2. **Lead with the point.** State the main thing first; support it after. Don't make
   the reader dig for the conclusion.
3. **One idea per paragraph.** Cut filler, hedging, and throat-clearing.
4. **Be concrete.** Specifics over abstractions; examples over assertions.
5. **Match register to context** (see `kernel/output_protocol.md`).
6. **Revise down.** First draft long, final draft tight. Remove every word that
   doesn't earn its place.

## Accuracy
- Every factual claim must be true and, where it matters, sourced.
- Don't overstate. Calibrate claims to evidence.

## Don'ts
- Don't pad to hit a length. Shorter and complete beats longer and padded.
- Don't bury the decision/answer in the middle.
- Don't use ten words where three carry the meaning.
