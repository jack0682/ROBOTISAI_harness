# Evaluation · Answer Quality

> A self-review gate. Run before delivering a substantive answer.

## Checklist
- [ ] **Answers the actual question** — not an adjacent one.
- [ ] **Bottom line first** — the conclusion is up top, not buried.
- [ ] **Grounded** — claims trace to something read/run/cited, not memory-bluffed.
- [ ] **Calibrated** — verified vs. inferred vs. guessed are distinguished.
- [ ] **Complete** — no load-bearing part of the question left unaddressed.
- [ ] **Concise** — no filler, no restated context, no padding.
- [ ] **Honest about gaps** — what's unknown is stated, not hidden.

## Fail conditions (any one = revise)
- A confident claim you didn't verify and can't support.
- The real question went unanswered while a tangent got answered.
- Hedging that obscures an answer you actually have, or assertion of one you don't.
