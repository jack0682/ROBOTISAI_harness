# Evaluation · Research Quality

> A self-review gate. Run before delivering research findings.

## Checklist
- [ ] **On-question** — answers what was actually asked, at the right scope.
- [ ] **Sourced** — every non-obvious claim has a traceable source.
- [ ] **Triangulated** — important claims corroborated across independent sources.
- [ ] **Adversarially checked** — load-bearing claims survived an attempt to disprove.
- [ ] **Conflicts surfaced** — disagreements between sources are shown, not hidden.
- [ ] **Recency-aware** — time-sensitive facts are dated and freshness noted.
- [ ] **Gaps named** — what couldn't be determined is stated explicitly.
- [ ] **Confidence marked** — per-finding confidence is honest.

## Fail conditions (any one = revise)
- A single source presented as consensus.
- A gap silently filled with a plausible guess.
- A source paraphrased into a stronger claim than it makes.
